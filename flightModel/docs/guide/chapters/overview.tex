\chapter{Overview}\label{ch:overview}

\section{Purpose of the workflow}

The workflow calculates the drag coefficient $C_D(M, \alpha)$ of a finned rocket at
the flight conditions of a real flight. It runs each condition on a series of meshes
to estimate the discretization error, and it records the cost of each run so that
another solver can be compared on the same cases.

The mesh must resolve three flow features. Their order is the order of their
contribution to the drag error:

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Shock waves} at transonic and supersonic speeds. The wave drag depends
  on the position and the strength of the shocks.
\item \textbf{The base wake.} The base pressure sets the base drag, a large part of the
  total drag of a rocket with a blunt base.
\item \textbf{The wall shear stress.} It sets the friction drag. It depends on the
  resolution of the boundary layer, that is, on \yplus.
\end{enumerate}

Flow features that do not change the axial force, such as the tip vortex of the fins,
do not get refinement.

\section{Workflow}

  \centering

  \label{fig:process}

\begin{steps}
\item \textbf{Geometry.} \file{mesh/makeRocket.py} writes the surface from the
  dimensions of the rocket (\cref{ch:geometry}).
\item \textbf{Mesh.} \file{mesh/Allmesh} makes one octree mesh; \file{runMesh.sh}
  makes the series of meshes of a study (\cref{ch:mesh}).
\item \textbf{Cases.} \file{newCase.sh} makes a case from a template, a mesh and a
  flight condition; \file{Allrun} runs it; \file{runCase.sh} does this for each line of
  a sweep file (\cref{ch:cases,ch:sweeps}).
\item \textbf{Results.} Each run records its coefficients, its samples and its cost
  (\cref{ch:results}). The post-processing compares the runs with the flight
  (\cref{ch:flight}) and with another solver (\cref{ch:nektar}).
\end{steps}
