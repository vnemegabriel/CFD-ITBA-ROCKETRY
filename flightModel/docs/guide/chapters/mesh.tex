\chapter{Mesh}\label{ch:mesh}

\section{Mesh generator}

The mesh generator is cfMesh (\cmd{cartesianMesh}). It makes an octree mesh from
one maximum cell size. All other sizes are refinement levels. Each level divides
the cell size by 2:
\[
  \Delta = \frac{\texttt{maxCellSize}}{2^{\,\text{level}}}
\]

\begin{table}[htbp]
\centering
\caption{Cell size of each level with \texttt{maxCellSize 1.6}.}\label{tab:levels}
\begin{tabular}{@{}cccccccc@{}}
\toprule
Level & 2 & 4 & 5 & 6 & 7 & 8 & 9 \\
Size [mm] & 400 & 100 & 50 & 25 & 12.5 & 6.25 & 3.13 \\
\midrule
Level & 10 & 11 & & & & & \\
Size [mm] & 1.56 & 0.78 & & & & & \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

A level has an effect only on its patch or its region. If a level is incorrect, the
cells in that region are too large or too small. No other region changes.

\subsection{Procedure}

\begin{steps}
\item Run \cmd{mesh/Allmesh <mesh-dir>} with the options of the mesh.
\item Read the summary at the end of the output. It gives the number of cells, the
  cell types, the maximum non-orthogonality, the maximum skewness and the failed
  checks.
\item If the output shows \val{negative cell volumes}, do not use the mesh. Change
  \file{meshDict} and make a new mesh in a new directory.
\item Measure the first cell on the walls (\cref{sec:layers}):
\begin{shell}
./post/firstLayer.py <mesh-dir>
\end{shell}
\item Do the checks of \cref{sec:examine-mesh}.
\end{steps}

\begin{caution}
A mesh with negative cell volumes is not usable. The other checks of
\ent{-allGeometry} that fail on these meshes (small determinant in the layer cells,
face tets, concave cells) are of a different category and do not stop the use of
the mesh.
\end{caution}

\subsection{Function of the .fms file}

An STL file contains only triangles and solid names. It does not contain edges. The
\file{.fms} file contains the triangles, the patches and the feature edges.
\cmd{surfaceFeatureEdges} marks an edge as a feature edge when the angle between the
two adjacent triangles is more than $60^\circ$. \cmd{cartesianMesh} then moves the
mesh vertices onto these edges.

The rocket has no sharp edge: the fin edges, the fin root, the shoulder and the base
edge are rounded, and each round has facets of less than $30^\circ$. With a limit of
$30^\circ$, small triangles where the root fillet meets the body gave 172 false
feature edges, and \cmd{cartesianMesh} bent the cells along them. With $60^\circ$,
only the edges of the domain box (12) and of the nose (88) remain.

a copy that was already written.
\end{caution}

\subsection{boundaryLayers}\label{sec:layers}

\begin{caution}
cfMesh splits the layer only on the patches that have an entry in
\ent{patchBoundaryLayers}. With only the global \ent{nLayers 10}, the body got no
layers, and the faces of the domain got 10. Keep an entry for each wall patch.
\end{caution}

The layer rule is
\[
  t_1 = h \, \frac{r - 1}{r^{\,n} - 1},
\]
if the whole layer were one surface cell thick. \cmd{cartesianMesh} makes it
thinner: the measured first cell is 0.41 times the rule. The rule thus keeps $t_1$
equal on all meshes, but it does not give its value. \file{post/firstLayer.py
<mesh-dir>} measures it. It gives $y$, the distance from the cell centre to the wall
face (as \ent{yPlus} uses it), and $t_1 = 2y$ for each wall patch, weighted by the
face area.

\section{Domain}\label{sec:domain}

\file{Allmesh} step 4 sets the domain with \cmd{surfaceGenerateBoundingBox}, which
takes six distances in metres from the bounding box of the rocket. The upstream and
downstream distances are fixed in \file{Allmesh}. The lateral distances come from
the STL file: \file{Allmesh} calculates them so that the side walls are at
$|y| = |z| = \unit{11.775}{m}$ for any rocket. The box is thus symmetric about
$z = 0$, which the half mesh needs (\cref{sec:half-mesh}).

\subsection{Half mesh}\label{sec:half-mesh}

At $\alpha = 0$ the flow has the symmetry of the fins. At an angle of attack in the
$x$--$y$ plane, the plane $z = 0$ stays a plane of symmetry. By default
\file{Allmesh} meshes the whole rocket with the fins at $45^\circ$ and keeps the half
$z \geq 0$.

\begin{steps}
\item The box is symmetric about $z = 0$. The octree thus splits on $z = 0$, and the
  cells of the full mesh do not cross it, except approximately 0.1\,\% near the wall.
\item \cmd{topoSet} selects the cells with the centre at $z \geq 0$, and
  \cmd{subsetMesh} keeps them. The faces of the cut go to \val{oldInternalFaces}.
\item The smoothing of \cmd{cartesianMesh} leaves the cut up to \unit{1.7}{mm} off the
  plane. \file{snapPlane.py} sets $z = 0$ on each point of the cut.
\item \file{createPatchDictHalf} makes the cut the patch \val{symm}, of type
  \val{symmetry}.
\item \file{meshInfo} gets \ent{sector half}, \ent{symmetryFraction 0.5} and
  $\texttt{Aref} = \pi r_\text{body}^2/2$. The coefficients are thus those of the
  full rocket. \file{Allrun} then permits $\alpha$ but not $\beta$.
\end{steps}

The half mesh takes the meshing time and the memory of the full mesh, because
\cmd{cartesianMesh} makes the full mesh.

\begin{caution}
Do not mesh $z \geq 0$ directly with \ent{zMin} on $z = 0$. \cmd{cartesianMesh} then
treats \ent{zMin} as a domain face that touches the wall. It gives \ent{zMin} the
layers of the wall, and the wall layers fan out at the corner: 9\,002 faces with
interpolation weight below 0.05 on $h1$.
\end{caution}

\section{Examine a mesh}\label{sec:examine-mesh}

\begin{steps}
\item Read the summary of \file{Allmesh}. There must be no negative cell volume.
\item Examine the patch names and types in \file{constant/polyMesh/boundary}.
  \val{cone}, \val{walls}, \val{tail} and \val{fins} must be \val{wall}.
\item Measure $t_1$ with \file{post/firstLayer.py}. The median on \val{walls} must
  agree with \cref{tab:meshes}.
\item Open the mesh in ParaView. Look at the cells on the fin edges, on the base edge
  and on the boattail shoulder.
\item Compare the number of cells with \cref{tab:mesh-quality}.
\end{steps}
